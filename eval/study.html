Usability study kit

Runs a small counterbalanced usability study of the HKBK chatbot against ordinary website browsing: consent, 6 timed tasks, the standard 10-question SUS, and a CSV export. Everything stays on this device until the proctor downloads the CSV.

This kit collects data from people. Get faculty/ethics approval before using it with real students, and only report numbers from real sessions - never from a dry run.
