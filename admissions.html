Admissions guide

Three gates in. One campus.

Every B.E. seat at HKBK comes through one of three routes: KCET, COMEDK, or the management quota. Here is the whole path, eligibility to enrolment, in one place.

The routes.

Karnataka quotaKCET

State counselling through KEA. HKBK's KCET code is E083 — use it during option entry. Indicative fee: roughly Rs 60,000 to Rs 1,00,000 per year.*

Lowest-fee route

All-India quotaCOMEDK

Private-college consortium counselling, open to candidates from any state. Fees sit noticeably above KCET and below most management seats.*

Open nationwide

DirectManagement quota

Apply straight to the college. Indicative figures put a Computer Science seat around Rs 2.83 to 3.25 lakh per year.* Confirm the current structure with the admissions office.

Fastest route

* Fee figures are third-party estimates and published totals disagree with one another. Treat them as a rough guide — the admissions office has the exact current structure and payment schedule.

Apply in four steps.

Apply online

Submit the application at apply.hkbk.edu.in.

Send your records

Marksheets and your entrance rank card (KCET / COMEDK, where applicable).

Verify documents

Attend document verification with the originals listed below.

Confirm the seat

Pay the fee to lock your seat. Ask admissions for the current deadline.

Eligibility — B.E.

  • 10+2 or equivalent, with Physics and Mathematics compulsory
  • Plus one of Chemistry, Biology, Biotechnology, Computer Science or Electronics
  • At least 45% aggregate (40% for reserved categories)
  • Valid KCET or COMEDK score for those quotas
  • Diploma holders: lateral entry straight into second year, via Karnataka DCET counselling

Eligibility — MBA

  • Bachelor's degree with at least 50% aggregate
  • Admission through PGCET or the management quota

Documents you will need

  • 10th and 12th marksheets
  • Transfer certificate
  • Migration certificate, where applicable
  • Entrance rank card
  • Caste or category certificate, if claiming reservation
  • Aadhaar
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Study certificate

International applicants additionally need passport and visa documents plus an equivalence certificate.

Talk to a human.

The admissions office answers eligibility, fees and entrance-route questions directly. Cutoffs move every year with the applicant pool, so for the latest closing ranks and deadlines, ask them — or ask our campus assistant on the home page.
