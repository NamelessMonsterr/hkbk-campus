01 — About

Twenty-eight years in north Bengaluru.

Founded in 1997 by Sri C. M. Ibrahim under the Karnataka Sunni Muslim Foundation, HKBK grew from a first intake of 45 computer science students to an approved undergraduate intake of 840.

Affiliation
Visvesvaraya Technological University, Belagavi

AICTE, New Delhi

NBA accredited programmes; NAAC accredited
Campus

50+, including an AICTE IDEA Lab

03 — Admissions

Four ways in.
