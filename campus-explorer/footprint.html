System footprint

What the chatbot costs to ship and to run, measured in your browser right now. Nothing is sent anywhere. See also the evaluation page.

What a visitor downloads

Transfer and uncompressed sizes come from the browser's own resource timing for this page load. On a repeat visit with a warm HTTP cache the transfer reads near zero - that is the cache doing its job, not a smaller site, and the uncompressed size stays honest.

Answer latency (measured now)

300 real queries through the engine in this tab: 6 canned questions, 50 rounds, timed with performance.now(). No server, no network, no external model - the whole pipeline (tokenise, fuzzy repair, TF-IDF retrieval, calibrated refusal) runs in the page.

Knowledge base freshness

Dates are when each entry was last changed in the project repository, as stated in the KB itself - not a re-verification with the college. Entries whose figures have conflicting published values are listed with every published value; the chatbot answers them as a sourced range, never as one confident number.

The whole system, in files

Retrieval engine, UI, and the full knowledge base. There is no backend component: these files plus any static host are the entire deployment.
