About Recording 2

Recording 2 is an independent Aug 22 preview: 211 still frames sampled from 20 source MOV clips and arranged by the supplied chapters. The frame view identifies its source clip and approximate source time so the preview remains auditable.

Always expect discontinuity. A new clip can begin inside a presentation chapter, and each such boundary is marked in the viewer with a hard cut rather than a crossfade. The images are ordinary portrait video frames, not panoramic or free-look coverage.
