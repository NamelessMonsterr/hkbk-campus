About this walkthrough

These are not renders. Every image is a frame sampled directly from phone video walked on campus (video/video/ in this project), scaled down and saved as a JPEG sequence in assets/tour-photos/. Nothing here is a 3D model, and nothing is generated — clicking forward advances to the next real photo in the sequence.

What it is not: not a single continuous take — it is five source clips across four presentation chapters (gate, entering, approach, around the Main Block) placed in walking order, so there is a hard cut at each chapter boundary rather than a seamless walk. Not panoramic — the source is ordinary narrow-field video, not a 360° camera, so there is no drag-to-look-around at a single point, only forward and back. Not geo-accurate — frames are sampled at a fixed time interval, not a fixed distance, so a slow stretch of the walk yields more steps than a fast one.
