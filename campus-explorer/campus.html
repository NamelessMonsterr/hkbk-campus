This page needs a real browser 3D pipeline

Either your browser/device doesn't support WebGL2, you have reduced motion enabled, or (most likely if you just double-clicked this file) it was opened as file:// - ES module imports and the GLTF loader are blocked there by the browser for security reasons.

Serve the project folder locally, e.g. python3 -m http.server 8000, then open http://localhost:8000/campus.html.
